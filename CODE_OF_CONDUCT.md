# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the FactTTL community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults or personal attacks, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository's GitHub security/contact information. Do not report conduct concerns in public issue threads.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
